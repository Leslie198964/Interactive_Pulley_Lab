Pulley Systems

Explore the mechanics of different pulley setups. Learn how they change the direction of force and multiply your effort through mechanical advantage.

MA = 1

Fixed Pulley

Drag the green arrow downwards!

Direction Change

A fixed pulley does not multiply force. It changes the direction of the force. You pull down to lift the load up.

Distance & Force (1:1)

The effort force equals the load weight (F = W). The distance pulled equals the distance lifted (s = h).

MA = 2

Movable Pulley

Drag the green arrow UPWARDS!

Halves the Effort

Because two segments of rope support the movable pulley, the weight is shared. You only need half the force to lift it (F = W / 2).

Doubles the Distance

To raise the load by distance h, you must pull the rope twice as far (s = 2h).

MA = 2

Compound Pulley

Drag the green arrow downwards!

Best of Both Worlds

The fixed pulley lets you pull down (convenient), while the movable pulley halves the effort (F = W / 2).

Trade-off: Distance

Conservation of energy applies. Saving half the effort means you must pull the rope twice the distance the load rises (s = 2h).
